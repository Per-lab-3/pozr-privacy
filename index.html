Privacy Policy for Pozr

Effective Date: October 2026 • Published by UraPix Studio

Welcome to Pozr (“we”, “our”, or “us”), developed by UraPix Studio. Pozr is a photography pose guide and camera assistant application for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through 100% on-device operation.

This Privacy Policy explains how Pozr interacts with your device, handles permissions, and ensures your data never leaves your control.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Pozr does not collect, store, transmit, rent, or sell any personal information or device telemetry.

  • No User Account: No sign-in, login credentials, name, email address, or phone number are required to use the app.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases that receive your camera feed or photos.
  • No Trackers or Ads: Pozr does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking libraries.

2. Camera Permission & On-Device Processing

Pozr requests camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA) solely to display the live viewfinder, align pose silhouette outlines, and capture photos at your request.

  • Live Viewfinder: Camera frames are displayed directly on your device screen to assist with posture and framing.
  • Scene Recognition: Smart scene categorization (such as outdoor, café, beach, or street) runs 100% locally on your phone using Google ML Kit on-device models. Frames are processed transiently in memory and discarded immediately.
  • Zero Biometric Identification: Pozr does not extract, catalog, or store facial recognition templates, identity hashes, or biometric identifiers.

3. Media Storage & Photo Picker

  • Captured Photos: Photos taken using Pozr are written directly to your device’s public storage gallery in the Pictures/Pozr folder via the Android MediaStore API. These files remain entirely on your device.
  • Custom Pose Import: When you import an inspiration photo to create a custom pose outline, Pozr utilizes the standard Android System Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). This grants temporary read access exclusively to the single selected image without broad storage access. Subject segmentation runs locally on-device.

4. Local Preferences Storage

User preferences (such as favorite pose presets and camera ratio settings) are stored locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore. Clearing the app’s data in your device settings or uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data.

5. Third-Party Sharing

Because Pozr does not transmit any information off your device, no data is ever shared with, sold to, or accessible by third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

Pozr does not collect personal data from any users, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if necessary to reflect product or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact UraPix Studio via the developer support email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
