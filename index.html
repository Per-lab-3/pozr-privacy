100% On-Device Privacy

Privacy Policy for Pozr

Effective Date: October 2026 • Published by UraPix Studio

Welcome to Pozr (“we”, “our”, or “us”), developed by UraPix Studio. Pozr is an intuitive, real-time photography pose guide and camera assistant application for Android. We hold user privacy as an absolute priority. This Privacy Policy provides a clear and comprehensive explanation of how Pozr operates, how device permissions are utilized, and how your privacy is guaranteed.

1 Zero Personal Data Collection & No Account Required

Guaranteed: Pozr does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or rent any personal information whatsoever.
  • No Registration: You are never asked to create an account, register, or provide your email address, phone number, name, or password.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate external web servers or cloud databases that ingest your live camera feed, captured images, or personal metadata.
  • Zero Behavioral Tracking: Pozr contains no third-party advertising frameworks, commercial trackers, or behavioral profiling software.

2 Camera Permission & 100% On-Device AI Processing

Pozr requests access to your device’s camera (android.permission.CAMERA) solely for real-time camera viewfinder preview, pose overlay alignment, and photo capture.

  • Live Viewfinder Only: The camera feed is rendered directly to your screen so you or your photographer can align posture, angles, and framing against translucent pose silhouettes.
  • Smart Scene Recognition: Pozr automatically recognizes environmental backdrops (such as café, beach, outdoor nature, urban street, indoor, or vehicle) to recommend matching poses. This runs 100% locally on your smartphone via Google ML Kit on-device image labeling models. No camera frames ever leave your device memory.
  • No Biometric Data: Pozr does not identify faces, store facial templates, collect biometric identifiers, or recognize individual human identities.

3 Custom Silhouette Extraction & Zero-Permission Media Picker

Pozr allows you to create custom pose silhouettes from reference images of your choice.

  • System Photo Picker: When you import an inspiration photo to generate a custom pose outline, Pozr uses the standard Android System Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). This grants temporary access only to the single image you explicitly select, with zero broad storage or media permissions required.
  • On-Device Subject Segmentation: Subject cutout and outline generation are performed entirely in your device’s local RAM using on-device ML Kit models. The source image is never uploaded to external servers.
  • Saved Custom Outlines: Extracted custom silhouettes are stored locally in your app’s private internal storage directory and can be deleted at any time with a single tap.

4 Captured Photo Storage (Direct to Your Gallery)

  • Your Photos Belong to You: Any photo you capture with Pozr is written directly to your phone’s public media gallery in the Pictures/Pozr directory using Android’s modern Scoped Storage MediaStore API.
  • No Server Copies: Photos are stored strictly on your local device. Pozr has no capability to view, sync, or transmit your captured photos outside your device.

5 Local App Preferences & Data Deletion

Your user settings—such as favorited pose templates, chosen camera aspect ratio, and first-time onboarding flags—are stored purely locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore.

  • How to Erase: You have full control. Clearing the app’s storage cache via your Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > Pozr > Storage > Clear Data) or uninstalling the app permanently erases all locally saved preferences and custom outlines instantly.

6 Third-Party Data Sharing

Because Pozr operates entirely on-device and does not harvest or transmit any user data, we do not share, sell, or disclose user data to any third party, marketing partner, or analytics provider.

7 Children’s Privacy & Family Safety

Pozr is a safe, family-friendly utility camera tool. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions). The app operates without user logins or social feeds.

8 Policy Updates & Contact Us

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new app features or regulatory standards. The latest version will always be maintained at this URL with an updated effective date.

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at our developer support email listed on the Google Play Store developer page.
