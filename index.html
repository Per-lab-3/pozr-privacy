PRIVACY

Our privacy policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

This Pozr Privacy Policy describes how UraPix Studio (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles and processes information about you. This Privacy Policy applies to information when you access or use our mobile application, photography pose guide tools, smart scene recognition features, and related services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, our “Services”) and when you otherwise interact with us.

Pozr is engineered with a strict 100% on-device architecture. We believe your creative moments and personal photos belong solely to you. We do not collect, transmit, or store your photos or personal data on remote servers.

We may change this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy regularly to stay informed about our information practices and the choices available to you.

COLLECTION OF INFORMATION

The information processed about you depends on how you interact with our Services. In this section, we describe the categories of information processed and the sources of this information.

Information You Provide to Us

We do not collect personal information that you provide directly to us because Pozr does not require user registration. You do not create an account, provide an email address, enter your phone number, or provide login credentials to use Pozr.

Information Processed On-Device

When you use the camera viewfinder or align your posture with pose guides:

  • Live Viewfinder Frames: Frames captured by your device camera are processed exclusively within your device’s volatile memory (RAM) to render the preview and overlay pose silhouettes in real time. These frames are never transmitted to cloud servers.
  • Smart Scene Recognition: Pozr uses on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit) to suggest relevant pose categories (such as outdoor, café, urban, or portrait). All ML inference runs locally on your phone hardware. No images, video streams, or inferences are sent off your device.
  • No Biometric Data: Pozr does not create, scan, or retain facial recognition models, facial geometry templates, biometric hashes, or identity profiles.

USE OF INFORMATION

Because Pozr functions entirely locally on your device, any camera input or preference selection is used solely to provide core app functionality:

  • Display real-time pose guide overlays and silhouette lines to assist your photography framing.
  • Provide intelligent scene-based pose suggestions using local hardware inference.
  • Capture high-resolution photos upon shutter press and store them directly in your device’s photo gallery.
  • Remember your local preferences (such as favorite pose presets and camera aspect ratio).

CAMERA AND SENSOR PROCESSING

To use Pozr, the app requests access to your device camera (android.permission.CAMERA). This permission is strictly utilized to operate the live camera viewfinder and allow you to capture photos. The camera is active only while the app is in the foreground and you are actively viewing the camera screen. We do not access the camera in the background or when the app is closed.

MEDIA STORAGE AND PHOTO PICKER

Pozr handles media according to modern Android privacy standards:

  • Saving Photos: When you capture a photo, Pozr saves the image directly into your phone’s public gallery under the Pictures/Pozr folder via the Android MediaStore API. These files remain under your complete control.
  • Custom Pose Import: If you choose to import an inspiration photo to generate a custom pose outline, Pozr opens the standard Android System Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). This zero-permission mechanism ensures Pozr only receives temporary read access to the specific photo you choose. Pose extraction is executed locally on-device, and no photos are uploaded.

DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

We do not share, sell, rent, disclose, or transfer any personal information, camera data, or photos to third parties. We do not include third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking SDKs, or data brokers in our application.

YOUR CHOICES

You have full control over the permissions and data associated with Pozr on your device:

  • Camera Permission: You can grant or revoke camera access at any time through your device settings under Settings > Apps > Pozr > Permissions.
  • Local Preferences & Data: You can reset all saved favorite poses and preferences by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

RETENTION OF INFORMATION

Because we do not collect or upload your photos, videos, or personal data to external servers, we do not retain your information. Photos captured with Pozr are stored locally on your device and remain there until you choose to delete them.

CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Our Services do not collect personal information from any users, including children under the age of 13.
